— 03 · About R2 Living

Boutique scale, full commitment.

R2 Living is a development brand owned by R2 Capital, a Warsaw-based investment company. We're not a corporation building housing at mass scale — we're a team that knows every project inside out.

We build one project at a time. Not because we have to — because we choose to give every build our full attention. That means you speak directly with the investor, not a customer service department. One set of decisions to track. One place that truly matters.

We build in the quieter areas around Warsaw. Generous plots, good schools nearby, access to the city without its noise. The kind of neighborhood a family chooses, not one it settles for.

— ONE PROJECT, FULL ATTENTION

One investment at a time, so nothing gets lost in a crowd of other projects. Every decision, every detail passes through the same hands from day one to the handover of keys.

— YOU TALK TO THE INVESTOR, NOT A CALL CENTER

Progress updates, site visits, direct contact with the person actually making decisions. You're not waiting in line for a consultant — you're part of the build.

— SPECIFICATIONS with NO SURPRISES

Materials, tolerances, and house plans published from day one. No fine print, no surprises at handover.
