— 01 · Brand Overview 2 / 7

Built boutique.

Who we are

R2 Living is the residential development brand of R2 Capital, a Warsaw-based real estate investment company. We build modern flat-roof duplex homes in the quieter suburbs of Warsaw — two to four projects per year.

What makes us different

We build one project at a time. Small enough that nothing slips, deliberate enough that nothing is rushed. Every buyer gets direct access to the investor, progress updates, and a floor plan they can still shape before the trades arrive.

Who we build for

Families choosing a neighbourhood — not settling for one. People who want Kampinos forest and Vistula air without giving up a Warsaw commute. Buyers who value honesty: published specs, no fine print, no surprises at handover.

— 04 · Voice & Tone 6 / 7

Voice & tone.

R2 Living speaks like a knowledgeable friend who happens to build houses — not a salesperson, not a corporate brochure. Direct, calm, and genuinely confident without being boastful. We say what we mean and don't pad.

Direct

We cut to what matters. No filler, no fluff. Short sentences. Active verbs. "We build one project at a time" — not "Our business model is characterised by a focused approach."

Honest

We publish specs and materials from day one. Our copy matches our product. We don't overpromise or use superlatives we can't back up with a measurement.

Warm

We're human. We care about the neighbourhood — the schools nearby, the walk to the river, the kitchen light in the afternoon. We write about life, not just property.

Precise

We use the right number, the exact measurement, the correct material name. When something is 36.72 m² we say 36.72 m². Precision is a form of respect.

— 05 · Content Themes & Checklist 7 / 7

Content themes & checklist.

Content theme pillars
Construction progress

Weekly or bi-weekly. Show the building going up — framing, screed, windows being fitted. Use real measurements. Caption: what stage, what's next.

Material & specification

Feature one material per post: the Porotherm block, triple-glazed frames, torch-on felt roof. Photograph the texture. Explain why it matters.

Neighbourhood & location

The Vistula in the morning. The Kampinos forest edge. A quiet Łomianki street. Show why someone would choose to live here.

Floor plan & configurator

Explain the layout — which rooms face which direction, why the kitchen is where it is. Show the configurator: "Power outlets before the electrician arrives."

Behind the scenes

Site visits. Investor conversations. The architect on site. Show the care behind the product without staging anything.

Community & delivery

Keys handed over. Family moving in. Garden in summer. Show that people actually live in R2 homes — and chose well.

Pre-publish checklist
Visual
Image is sharp, minimum 2400px on long edge
No heavy filters — colours read true to the building
R2 brand colours visible (Ink background, Moss accent, or Bone text)
No watermarks or third-party branding visible
Copy
First line works standalone (visible before "more")
No superlatives without evidence (luxury, premium, best)
Measurements in correct format: m², not sqm
Polish diacritics correct: Łomianki, Wspólna, Kiełpin
Hashtags at end or in first comment — not in body copy
CTA included and relevant to current project phase
