— 01 · Brand Overview 2 / 7

Built boutique.

Who we are

R2 Living is the residential development brand of R2 Capital, a Warsaw-based real estate investment company. We build modern flat-roof duplex homes in the quieter suburbs of Warsaw — two to four projects per year.

What makes us different

We build one project at a time. Small enough that nothing slips, deliberate enough that nothing is rushed. Every buyer gets direct access to the investor, progress updates, and a floor plan they can still shape before the trades arrive.

Who we build for

Families choosing a neighbourhood — not settling for one. People who want Kampinos forest and Vistula air without giving up a Warsaw commute. Buyers who value honesty: published specs, no fine print, no surprises at handover.

— 04 · Voice & Tone 6 / 7

Voice & tone.

R2 Living speaks like a knowledgeable friend who happens to build houses — not a salesperson, not a corporate brochure. Direct, calm, and genuinely confident without being boastful. We say what we mean and don't pad.

Direct

We cut to what matters. No filler, no fluff. Short sentences. Active verbs. "We build one project at a time" — not "Our business model is characterised by a focused approach."

Honest

We publish specs and materials from day one. Our copy matches our product. We don't overpromise or use superlatives we can't back up with a measurement.

Warm

We're human. We care about the neighbourhood — the schools nearby, the walk to the river, the kitchen light in the afternoon. We write about life, not just property.

Precise

We use the right number, the exact measurement, the correct material name. When something is 36.72 m² we say 36.72 m². Precision is a form of respect.

— 05 · Logo 7 / 7

The logo.

The R2 Living identity consists of two elements: the full wordmark — used for primary brand contexts — and the R2 symbol mark, used where space is limited or the brand is already established.

Minimum size

Full wordmark: 80px wide minimum on screen, 28mm in print. Symbol mark: 24px minimum on screen.

Clear space

Maintain clear space equal to the height of the "R" on all sides. Nothing should intrude on this zone.

Approved colours

Ink (#14120F) on light backgrounds. Bone (#F3EFE8) on dark or Moss backgrounds. No other colour combinations.

Never do

Do not rotate, stretch, recolour, add effects, or place on a busy background without adequate contrast.
